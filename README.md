# Ansh Healthcare

### Official Website & Personal Development Project

Ansh Healthcare is the official website of **Ansh Healthcare**, a pharmaceutical healthcare and wholesale distribution company.

This repository contains the source code for the official Ansh Healthcare website. The project is also maintained as a personal development project, where the website is continuously improved, modernized, and maintained with a focus on performance, responsive design, product presentation, and business enquiries.

## 🏥 About Ansh Healthcare

Ansh Healthcare is focused on pharmaceutical products, healthcare distribution, and business opportunities for wholesalers, distributors, franchises, and healthcare partners.

The website provides a public-facing platform for:

- Presenting the Ansh Healthcare brand
- Showcasing the pharmaceutical product portfolio
- Organizing products by therapeutic categories
- Providing detailed product information
- Sharing the complete product portfolio
- Generating wholesale and distribution enquiries
- Providing franchise and distributor opportunities
- Connecting prospective business partners with Ansh Healthcare
